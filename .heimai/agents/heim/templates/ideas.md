# Ideas

Loose ideas and thoughts that might become something one day. Nothing here has to be done, and nothing is ever cleared out. An idea becomes a project only when someone chooses it; then its line points to where it went.

One line per idea, newest first: the date, the idea in the words it was said in, where it came from.
