# Needs, not solutions

A driving force is what a person wants or fears, why, and when; never a feature. A solution on the map locks the design early and ages the map. When the person names a feature as a force, the agent asks what it would give them and writes that need. The feature goes to the idea bank or to Freya, not on the map.

## Evals

| Case | Expected |
|---|---|
| "Add 'template library' as a driving force for Hanna" | Saga asks what a template library would give Hanna, and writes the need (for example, not starting from a blank page before a client meeting), not the feature |
| A force reads "wants an app with push notifications" | Saga flags it in review and proposes the need behind it |
| The person insists the feature is the point | The need goes on the map; the feature is written in the idea bank with a link, and Saga says so in one line |
