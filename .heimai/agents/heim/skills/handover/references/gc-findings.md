# G&C at the wrap: incidents, deviations, gaps

Governance says what applies and why: the principles, the policies and who decides. Compliance is how we keep to them: routines, checks, deviations and incidents. Any session can show the framework lacks something, and the wrap catches it.

## What to look for

- **Incident:** something happened that breaks a principle, or nearly did (a password the project uses turns up in a public leak).
- **Deviation:** something in the repo or the way of working does not follow a principle right now.
- **Gap:** a case the policy does not cover, so the agent had to improvise.

## For each finding

1. **Urgent first.** An exposed secret or ongoing damage is said when it is found, not at the wrap. The person acts; a key is rotated in the password manager.
2. **Report it** in the incident log the governance folder's `policies.md` names for the right level. A folder with a `.source` is a synced copy: the report goes to the source it names, or as a handover to the policy's owner when the agent cannot write there. Write the date, what happened, the impact, what was done and who knows. **Never the secret itself**, only where it was and whether it has been changed.
3. **Propose a point:** a routine (compliance) or a principle that is missing (governance), marked `proposal`, dated, linked to the incident.
4. **The mandate:** an agent may report, propose and tighten. It never loosens or removes a principle, or widens what agents may do, on its own; that takes the policy owner's yes. Idun included.
5. **A Heim with no governance** (a private Heim, decision 15): the soul files are the rules; write the finding under `## G&C` in the work part.

Nothing found: skip the step and say "G&C: nothing new" in the receipt.
